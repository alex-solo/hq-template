## Invoice-chaser for freelance translators

- **stage:** researched · **origin:** founder
- **Evidence:** 30+ threads on r/TranslatorsCafe about late payment;
  existing tools (Chaser, Kolleno) start at €40/month and target
  SMB finance teams, not solo freelancers; ProZ job board shows ~600
  active translators in the founder's language pair.
- **Strongest case against:** freelancers' payment problem is
  collection, not reminders — a reminder tool changes nothing about a
  client who won't pay. Willingness to pay is unproven: no freelancer in
  the threads names a tool they pay for today.
- **Validation cost:** one week — a landing page plus five interviews
  with translators from the threads above.
- **Mentor's challenge (2026-02-14):** incomplete until an interview
  shows someone already paying (or paying with hours) for the problem.
