## ferry-watch (`~/code/business/ventures/ferry-watch`)

- **Thesis:** Ferry commuters on the Tagus lose 20–40 min/week to
  unannounced cancellations; a €3/month SMS alert sourced from the
  operator's status page and crowd reports is worth more than the coffee
  it costs.
- **Stage (2026-03-02):** MVP live, 41 free users, billing not wired.
- **Revenue:** €0. First paying user is the next milestone.
- **Kill criteria (set 2026-01-20):** fewer than 10 paying users by
  2026-06-30 → mandatory mentor review; operator publishes a free
  official alert channel → personal-tool mode within 4 weeks.
- **Mentor's current read:** execution risk is gone; all remaining risk
  is demand-side. Next founder hours go to billing and one distribution
  channel (the commuter Facebook group), not to features.
