# Portfolio rules — founder directives that bind every venture

Nothing recorded yet. Single source of the founder's standing rules;
`ventures/CLAUDE.md` imports it for every venture session.
When the founder states a rule in one venture, it goes here the same
day, dated, in his words — venture files and agent memories keep only
venture-specific facts. `example/rules.md` shows the filled-in shape.
Its history goes in `rules-changelog.md`, which no session loads.
